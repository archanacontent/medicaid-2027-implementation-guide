# Requirements

## Who It Applies To

<span class="mcd-label">Work requirements</span>
Non-pregnant adults aged 19 to 64 in the Medicaid expansion group or certain section 1115 demonstrations, who aren't enrolled in or entitled to Medicare. CMS says the rule affects 43 states and D.C.

<span class="mcd-label">Six-month renewals</span>
Adults covered through Medicaid expansion, whether through the state plan or a waiver covering the whole expansion population. Other groups, such as children, parents and pregnant or postpartum people, keep 12-month renewals, and so do American Indian and Alaska Native enrollees.

## What Members Must Show

<span class="mcd-label">Qualifying activities</span>
At least [80 hours a month](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/medicaid-community-engagement-requirement-certain-individuals-interim-final-rule-comment-period-cms) of work, community service or a work program, or enrollment in education at least half-time. Members can combine activities to reach the total.

<span class="mcd-label">Income instead of hours</span>
Monthly income of at least the federal minimum wage times 80 hours, which is $580 a month in 2026, also meets the requirement.

<span class="mcd-label">When it's checked</span>
Applicants must show compliance for one to three consecutive months before the month they apply, with the number of months set by the state. Enrolled members must show at least one compliant month in each eligibility period, and states may check more often.

## Who Is Exempt

The [interim final rule](https://rarediseases.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/NORD_RANExplainer_CMS__2454_IFC_CERequirements_2026.pdf) lists categories that states must exclude:

- **Former Foster Youth** - who turned 18 on or after January 1, 2023.
- **American Indians And Alaska Natives** - regardless of income or activity.
- **Parents And Caregivers** - of a child aged 13 or under, or of a person with a disability.
- **Disabled Veterans** - with a total disability rating from the VA.
- **Medically Frail Individuals** - including people with serious mental illness, substance use disorder, or physical, intellectual or developmental disabilities.
- **Members Already Meeting Other Program Rules** - people meeting TANF work requirements, or SNAP household members not subject to SNAP work rules.
- **People In Treatment** - in a drug or alcohol treatment and rehabilitation program.
- **Incarcerated Individuals** - in a public institution.
- **Pregnant And Postpartum Individuals** - through the end of the postpartum period.

States may also offer short-term hardship exceptions for inpatient care, federally declared disasters, travel for medical care, and counties with high unemployment, where unemployment is at least 8% or 1.5 times the national rate, whichever is lower.
