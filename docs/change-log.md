# Change Log

## Regulatory Change Log

<ul class="mcd-log">
<li><span class="date">July 4, 2025</span><span class="mcd-tag">New</span><br>H.R. 1 becomes law, creating the community engagement requirement and six-month renewals for expansion adults.</li>
<li><span class="date">March 6, 2026</span><span class="mcd-tag">Guidance</span><br>CMS issues a State Medicaid Director Letter on six-month renewals and how to move to them.</li>
<li><span class="date">June 1, 2026</span><span class="mcd-tag">New</span><br>CMS issues interim final rule CMS-2454-IFC on community engagement.</li>
<li><span class="date">July 31, 2026</span><span class="mcd-tag">Deadline</span><br>Public comments on the interim final rule close.</li>
<li><span class="date">July 1 to September 1, 2026</span><span class="mcd-tag">Deadline</span><br>Advance outreach notices are due, depending on each state's look-back period.</li>
<li><span class="date">January 1, 2027</span><span class="mcd-tag">Effective</span><br>Work requirements and six-month renewals begin.</li>
<li><span class="date">December 31, 2028</span><span class="mcd-tag">Deadline</span><br>All good-faith exemptions that delay a state's start expire.</li>
</ul>
