# Change Log

## Regulatory Change Log

<ul class="mcd-log">
<li><span class="date">July 4, 2025</span><span class="mcd-tag">New</span><br>H.R. 1 is signed into law. It creates the community engagement requirement and moves expansion adults to six-month renewals.</li>
<li><span class="date">March 6, 2026</span><span class="mcd-tag">Guidance</span><br>CMS sends states a State Medicaid Director Letter on six-month renewals, including the two ways to transition.</li>
<li><span class="date">June 1, 2026</span><span class="mcd-tag">New</span><br>CMS publishes interim final rule CMS-2454-IFC on community engagement.</li>
<li><span class="date">July 31, 2026</span><span class="mcd-tag">Deadline</span><br>The comment period on the interim final rule closes.</li>
<li><span class="date">July 1 to September 1, 2026</span><span class="mcd-tag">Deadline</span><br>Advance outreach notices fall due, with each state's date set by its look-back period.</li>
<li><span class="date">October 29, 2026</span><span class="mcd-tag">Ruling</span><br>A federal court strikes down part of the medically frail definition in the interim final rule.</li>
<li><span class="date">January 1, 2027</span><span class="mcd-tag">Effective</span><br>Work requirements and six-month renewals take effect.</li>
<li><span class="date">December 31, 2028</span><span class="mcd-tag">Deadline</span><br>Every good-faith exemption that delays a state's start runs out.</li>
</ul>
