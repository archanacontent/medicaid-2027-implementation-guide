# Medicaid's January 2027 Double Deadline

<p class="sub">An Implementation Guide For Work Requirements And Six-Month Renewals</p>

<p class="lede">On January 1, 2027, state Medicaid programs take on two federal mandates at the same time. Adults in the expansion population must demonstrate compliance with a new <a href="https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/medicaid-community-engagement-requirement-certain-individuals-interim-final-rule-comment-period-cms">community engagement requirement</a>, and the same population moves to <a href="https://shvs.org/resources/new-cms-guidance-on-six-month-renewals-in-medicaid/">eligibility renewals every six months</a>. Each change adds verification steps, notices and caseworker decisions to processes that are already stretched. The 2023–24 unwinding showed where that pressure breaks: <a href="https://www.kff.org/medicaid/how-did-medicaid-renewal-outcomes-change-during-the-unwinding/">most coverage losses were procedural</a>, driven by missed documentation rather than ineligibility. For state agencies and their technology partners, readiness depends on systems that verify through existing data, notices members can act on and procedures caseworkers can apply consistently, all in place before the deadline arrives.</p>

## The Problem

<span class="mcd-label">What changed</span>
[H.R. 1, signed July 4, 2025](https://rarediseases.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/NORD_RANExplainer_CMS__2454_IFC_CERequirements_2026.pdf), made work, study or community service a condition of Medicaid eligibility for many expansion adults, and doubled how often their eligibility is checked.

<span class="mcd-label">What breaks</span>
Both changes add paperwork, and paperwork is where people actually lose coverage. During the 2023–24 Medicaid unwinding, [roughly 24 million people were disenrolled](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/how-did-medicaid-renewal-outcomes-change-during-the-unwinding/), and between 66% and 74% of those losses were procedural: a missed form or an unanswered notice, not a finding that the person no longer qualified.

<span class="mcd-label">What it costs</span>
CMS itself [projects about 2.3 million coverage losses](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/06/cms-issues-interim-final-rule-implementing-medicaid-community) in 2027 from the new requirement. How many of those come from people who actually meet the rules but can't prove it in time depends largely on how clearly states verify, notify and explain.
